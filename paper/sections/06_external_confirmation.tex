\section{External Confirmation: What We Tried}
\label{sec:external}

A discovery claim of this shape---``the model cannot update''---is most
threatened by the objection ``it just cannot see.'' We therefore report, as
primary evidence rather than appendix filler, an unusually wide set of failed
removals. Each was a live hypothesis with a falsification bar; each failed,
and each failure narrows what the defect can be:

\paragraph{Denser supervision and geometry readouts.} Dense per-point labels,
slope supervision along paths, SDF-valued readouts, and distilling boundary
proximity into the student all fail to remove the miss (E7 falsification net:
12 arms $\times$ 5 seeds, 60 trainings; flip-mix, SDF, tangent, anti-flatness,
coupled-margin, vanilla-mixup, and BAN all lose to or tie the flipmine
baseline). Slopes are not the binding constraint.

\paragraph{Architectures and curricula.} Relational variants (up to 1200-epoch
deconfounded runs), attention readouts, margin--SupCon hybrids, and quartet
curricula (pixel arm: emergent $0.572$, worse than clean $0.537$) all fail to
fix the update. What looks like ``more compositional'' training data can hurt.

\paragraph{Interchange patch (X03, rank-4 narrowed run x03b).} Hybrid truth
(donor.AB + base.CD) decodes from concatenated frozen hidden states at
readout anti-copy accuracy $0.87$--$0.89$ (clean/flipmine, $n{=}150$ anti-only),
while the learned rank-4 patch map installed into the frozen classifier at the
tested layer scores interchange-anti $0.27$/$0.23$ (random-subspace anti
$0.15$/$0.13$; full-swap overall $0.47$/$0.51$ on $n{=}450$). Supported claim:
decodable $\neq$ installable by the tested patch at this layer. Not supported:
any statement of the form ``the representation fundamentally cannot
compose.'' No rank/layer search was opened; the negative stands as scoped.

\paragraph{Thresholds and temperatures.} Dev-selected thresholds and fitted
temperatures do not rescue the feasible set (Sec.~\ref{sec:repair}); the
problem is in the probabilities themselves, not the cutoff.

Recorded pattern: the model sees the parts (atomic accuracy is high),
responds to change (temperature interventions move $10$--$50\%$ of action
selections with argmax fixed), and still fails to update on the hard
constructions---while the failures above delimit, rather than explain, the
defect.
