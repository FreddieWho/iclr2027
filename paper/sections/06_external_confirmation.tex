\section{Failed Removals and Scoped Negatives}
\label{sec:failures}

Each entry was a live hypothesis with a falsification bar; each failed, and
each failure narrows what the defect can be. None is used to claim that all
mechanisms of a family are excluded.

\paragraph{Denser supervision and geometry readouts.} Dense per-point
labels, slope supervision along paths, SDF-valued readouts, and distilling
boundary proximity into the student all fail to remove the miss (E7
falsification net: 12 arms $\times$ 5 seeds, 60 trainings; flip-mix, SDF,
tangent, anti-flatness, coupled-margin, vanilla-mixup, and BAN all lose to
or tie the flipmine baseline). Slopes are not the binding constraint.

\paragraph{Architecture and curriculum variants (earlier relation
variants).} Before the relational-input result of
Sec.~\ref{sec:repair}, relation-pooling and attention-readout variants
(1200-epoch deconfounded runs), margin--SupCon hybrids, and quartet
curricula all failed on the same joint-consistency metrics (pixel arm:
emergent $0.572$, worse than clean $0.537$). These are architecture-level
variants; the label-free relational \emph{input} of
Sec.~\ref{sec:repair} is a different intervention and is reported
separately---its positive evidence does not retroactively absolve the
earlier variants, and their failure does not bound it.

\paragraph{Interchange patch (X03, rank-4 narrowed run x03b).} Hybrid
truth (donor.AB + base.CD) decodes from concatenated frozen hidden states
at readout anti-copy accuracy $0.87$--$0.89$ (clean/flipmine, $n{=}150$
anti-only), while the learned rank-4 patch map installed into the frozen
classifier at the tested layer scores interchange-anti $0.27$/$0.23$
(random-subspace anti $0.15$/$0.13$; full-swap overall $0.47$/$0.51$ on
$n{=}450$). Supported claim: decodable $\neq$ installable by the tested
patch at this layer. Not supported: any statement of the form ``the
representation fundamentally cannot compose.'' No rank/layer search was
opened; the negative stands as scoped.

\paragraph{Thresholds and temperatures (scope).} Argmax-preserving
temperature rescaling ($T>0$) changes no classification decision, so its
invariance properties are algebraic, not empirical exclusions; dev-selected
thresholds coinciding with the lexicographic rule at one operating point
share the same ranking by construction. The interface analysis of
Sec.~\ref{sec:interfaces} is therefore reported as coverage/precision
decomposition plus scale-sensitivity, not as an empirical elimination of
all calibration schemes.
