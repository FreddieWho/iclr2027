\section{Evaluation Protocol and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:repro}

\paragraph{One-shot fresh holdout.} \texttt{holdout\_909} was minted after all
discovery, pre-registered (E1\_PREREG), evaluated once in a single
uninterrupted run with fixed checkpoints, and archived as used; no new metric
has been or will be run on it. Discovery numbers and fresh numbers are never
averaged and always labeled with denominators.

\paragraph{Single evidence table.} Every number in the main text is drawn from
\texttt{reports/FINAL\_EVIDENCE\_TABLE.md}, which records numerator,
denominator, dataset, split, seeds, status, and artifact path per claim, plus
six wording red-lines (fresh-headline rule, F1 denominator discipline, X03
term ban, U02 anti-information retraction, U03b nuisance wording, football
holdout caveat). A smoke audit re-derived all headline numbers from artifacts.

\paragraph{Artifacts.} Code, scene pools, mined flips, checkpoints metadata,
rendering scripts, and SHA-256 manifests are versioned with the paper;
restricted-access football derivatives are described with hashes and
conversion receipts. Seeds 11/23/47 are used throughout; match is the unit of
independence on the football side.
