\section{Real-Data Confirmation: Football}
\label{sec:football}

The same three failure-and-repair shapes recur on IDSSE real football tracking
data, evaluated cross-field over three training seeds (s11/s23/s47):

\paragraph{Transitions.} Turn miss, frozen $\to$ covered: s11
$0.277 \to 0.129$, s23 $0.383 \to 0.184$, s47 $0.336 \to 0.176$---halved in all
three seeds, with integral and accuracy metrics moving the same way.

\paragraph{Events.} Conditional event miss, frozen $\to$ covered: s11
$0.255 \to 0.092$, s23 $0.362 \to 0.180$, s47 $0.347 \to 0.168$.

\paragraph{Actions.} Hard action-invalid rate ($\lambda{=}2$), frozen $\to$
covered: s11 $0.309 \to 0.102$, s23 $0.410 \to 0.184$, s47 $0.348 \to 0.141$;
at $\lambda{=}0$ all three models sit near zero.

No pristine football holdout exists, so this is three-seed same-direction
confirmation, not single-read proof; the coordinate fresh holdout carries the
confirmatory weight and football carries the external-reality weight, as the
per-claim labels state. Football supports transition
repair and event/action behavior; the confidence-inversion finding does
\emph{not} generalize here---natural-turn analyses show floor/null effects
after correction, so Sec.~3's confidence claim stays coordinate-only.
