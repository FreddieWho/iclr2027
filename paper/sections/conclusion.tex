\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We studied correctness under controlled change with oracle-known edits.
Atomic correctness does not guarantee tracking the joint outcome
($151/176$ fresh conditional misses); pre-change confidence orders
current-state risk classically but update risk in reverse, in coordinate
scenes; and endpoint repair mostly relocates errors rather than removing
them, until a bounded relational-input intervention shifts repair toward
full consistency on both banks. Static accuracy, endpoint correctness,
full compositional consistency, and downstream success are distinct
notions of reliability under change, and conflating them overstates both
models and repairs. An evaluation that separates them---with frozen banks,
stated denominators, and paired quartet accounting---is the paper's
practical takeaway.
