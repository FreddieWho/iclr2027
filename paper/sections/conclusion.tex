\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under oracle-known changes, atomic correctness can coexist with joint
failure ($151/176$ fresh conditional misses), and endpoint recovery can
relocate errors. Task-adapted relational inputs improve full joint
correctness across geometric tasks and matched finite training budgets,
but interaction signs depend on the task. Coordinate confidence inversion
does not transfer to natural football observations. Frozen banks, explicit
denominators, and paired state accounting separate these findings from
claims about universal architectures or mechanisms.
