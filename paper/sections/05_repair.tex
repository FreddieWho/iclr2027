\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Intervention-based evaluation has lineage in causal abstractions and
interchange analysis (activation swaps for causal-model alignment;
\citep{geiger2021causal}); ours differs in using input-space reallocation
and semantic composition as the prediction target. Sensitivity analysis
(Morris elementary effects; \citep{morris1991factorial}) supplies the
screening metaphor, but our screen is semantic: edits with known oracle
outcomes, not random directions.

On compositional generalization we do not originate conditional-on-atomics
measurement: the compositionality gap (correct sub-problems, failed
composition) is established for language models
\citep{press2023measuring}, with a stricter double-gated variant for
factual composition \citep{yu2026compositioncollapse}. Our differences:
(1)~controlled semantic change with known boundaries;
(2)~same-instance transition structure (paths, not just endpoints);
(3)~confidence meaning reversal under semantic flips;
(4)~repair error migration inside the same quartet; (5)~downstream
association. Hard-positive mining in vision--language composition (Kamath
et al., 2024) trades coverage against precision and can hurt the hard
positives it targets; our quartet analysis measures the analogous
trade-off inside the same instance rather than across disjoint positive
sets.

On calibration and selective classification
\citep{guo2017calibration,geifman2017selective}, and on the disparity
risks of confidence-based rejection more broadly (Jones et al., 2021),
our contrast is explicit: selective prediction concerns \emph{current}
correctness, while our inversion concerns reliability \emph{after} a true
semantic change, measured with known label transformations. Excessive
invariance is directly adjacent: \citet{jacobsen2019excessive} show that
classifiers can be invariant precisely along task-relevant directions of
variation, which is the failure family our transition blindness
instantiates under known edits; our object adds oracle-known composition
and the repair/migration accounting. Counterfactually augmented data
\citep{kaushik2020learning} and positive-congruent, regression-free model
updates \citep{yan2021positive} are the closest repair neighborhoods; we
claim no priority on preserve constraints, only the instance-level
migration measurement and the coverage/quality decomposition. Task-based
end-to-end learning in stochastic optimization (Donti et al., 2017)
bounds the decision-interface literature our P2 analysis touches; we use
fixed action libraries rather than learned surrogates. Label-free
relational features follow a long line of geometric invariance work
\citep{lenc2015understanding} and scalar-equivariant representation
building blocks (Villar et al., 2021); our contribution is the
joint-repair evidence under this input, not the features. Prior probing
literature \citep{belinkov2022probing} motivates our diagnostic
instruments but is background, not claim.
