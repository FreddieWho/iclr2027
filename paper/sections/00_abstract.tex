When does supervision of a single change improve full compositional
correctness, rather than only moving the error? On oracle-known coordinate
edits, two individually judged-correct edits still miss the joint change
in $151/176$ fresh-holdout cases. On the historical development bank, raw
flip coverage on the baseline-broken subset mostly relocates that error
(full repair $0.03$--$0.10$, migration $0.36$--$0.40$). That pattern is
not every repair. The same coverage with task-adapted relational inputs
raises joint consistency, and on the separate $509$-quartet confirmation
bank full repair exceeds migration. Input advantage is not positive
interaction transfer: the segment--disk joint-scale interaction is
negative. A standard ResNet-18 on a separate rendered bank is a boundary.
Under matched total exposure, static-to-flip training increases joint
correctness, and full repair exceeds migration on each fixed baseline-110
subset. Matched total exposure is not matched per-parent exposure.
A fresh-source analytic segment-intersection parser solves the coordinate
E bank, so the learned source comparisons are parser-bounded diagnostics
rather than a claim that a complex relation architecture is required.
Coordinate confidence inversion is a scoped side result, not the question.
