\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.} Three statements carry the paper.
First, under oracle-known controlled edits, atomic correctness does not
transfer to the joint outcome: the conditional composition miss is large,
stable across seeds and banks, and decomposes into missed per-step updates
rather than a single mysterious composed state. Second, a model's
pre-change confidence orders \emph{current-state} risk classically but
\emph{update} risk in reverse; this is a descriptive reliability
separation in coordinate scenes, not a causal claim that confidence causes
blindness, and it does not license claims of memory inertia or neural
network pathologies beyond the measured behavior. Third, endpoint repair
systematically overstates recovery: most endpoint gains are error
migration until the input representation changes. The relational-input
result is the bounded positive instance---it moves joint consistency and
reverses the migration pattern on the confirmation bank---but $J$ remains
below $0.5$, the features embed task-aware structure, and no general
architecture claim is made.

\paragraph{What we cannot exclude.} The affine operating-point control is
bounded (one seed fits at its coefficient grid edge), so ``not a pure
threshold shift'' is a statement about that control family, not about all
monotone recalibrations. The temperature analysis shows action rules react
to score scale; it does not identify what, if anything, the encoder
represents about the change. Failed architecture, curriculum, and
interchange-patch attempts (Appendix~\ref{sec:failures}) delimit the
defect without explaining it. The keepbal pilot bounds one fixed
sampling/weighting scheme, not positive-example protection in general.

\paragraph{Deployment reading.} The retention analysis is a
reweighting-of-a-selected-population result: it quantifies what
confidence-based filtering buys and what it concentrates, under a known
label transformation. It is not an estimate of deployment error rates,
where the change composition is unknown and the retained fraction is not
controlled.

\paragraph{Limitations (all evidenced, none hidden).}
(i)~P1 strong evidence is coordinate-only; natural football turns show
floor/null.
(ii)~Models are small task-trained models plus one small CNN family; the
foundation bridge never established atomic competence, so architecture
generality is untested.
(iii)~Synthetic coordinate scenes are not real tactical changes; distance
to tactical semantics is unmeasured.
(iv)~Football evidence is three-seed same-direction without a pristine
holdout; the single-read proof lives on the coordinate side.
(v)~Pixel cross-style repair rests on three training seeds, all
same-signed; absolute pixel levels are style-sensitive (color-swap
collapse reported); full compositional consistency is not measured in
pixels.
(vi)~The interchange-patch negative is scoped to one layer and one rank-4
patch form.
(vii)~Joint-consistency absolute values are low (single-digit to
$\sim$0.47); small real-data effects are reported without inflation; the
weakest seed stays in every table.
(viii)~Relational features are hand-designed and relation-aware by
construction; no universal mechanism is claimed.
(ix)~The dev-fitted affine control sits at its coefficient grid edge for
one seed (s11); affine conclusions are comparisons, not exact fits.
(x)~The confirmation bank has been reused across analyses (P1 first,
relational-arm evaluation later); usage is documented per claim
(Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}), and first-read status is claimed only where it
applies.
