\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
Atomic correctness, update reliability, and full repair differ under
oracle-known edits. Coordinate pre-change confidence ranks current risk
classically but update risk in reverse; this does not show that confidence
causes blindness. Relational inputs improve joint consistency and shift
endpoint recovery toward full repair. The original source four-arm study
remains below $J=.5$; optimized source models and new tasks reach higher
levels, with task-dependent interaction signs. None establishes an
architecture-wide mechanism or neural pathology.

\paragraph{What we cannot exclude.} The affine operating-point control is
bounded (one seed fits at its coefficient grid edge), so ``not a pure
threshold shift'' is a statement about that control family, not about all
monotone recalibrations. The temperature analysis shows action rules react
to score scale; it does not identify what, if anything, the encoder
represents about the change. Failed architecture, curriculum, and
interchange-patch attempts (Appendix~\ref{sec:failures}) delimit the
defect without explaining it. The keepbal pilot bounds one fixed
sampling/weighting scheme, not positive-example protection in general.

\paragraph{Deployment reading.} The retention analysis is a
reweighting-of-a-selected-population result: it quantifies what
confidence-based filtering buys and what it concentrates, under a known
label transformation. It is not an estimate of deployment error rates,
where the change composition is unknown and the retained fraction is not
controlled.

\paragraph{Limitations (all evidenced, none hidden).}
(i)~P1 strong evidence is coordinate-only; natural football turns show
floor/null.
(ii)~Models include small coordinate networks, a small CNN, and ResNet-18.
The new ResNet-18 comparison supports same-task joint repair under matched
total training budgets. Native multimodal generality remains untested.
(iii)~Synthetic coordinate scenes are not real tactical changes; distance
to tactical semantics is unmeasured.
(iv)~Football evidence is three-seed same-direction without a pristine
holdout; the single-read proof lives on the coordinate side.
(v)~Pixel cross-style repair rests on three training seeds, all
same-signed; absolute pixel levels are style-sensitive (color-swap
collapse reported); full repair versus migration is not established by that small-CNN study.
The later ResNet-18 study does report joint correctness.
(vi)~The interchange-patch negative is scoped to one layer and one rank-4
patch form.
(vii)~Source-task joint correctness remains below $0.5$ in the main
four-arm experiment; T1/T2 distance inputs reach approximately $.75$ and
$.998$, and the visual baseline $.71$--$.74$. These task-specific levels
are not interchangeable; collapsed seeds remain reported.
(viii)~Relational features are hand-designed and relation-aware by
construction; the symmetry account in Sec.~\ref{sec:repair} is a
description of these features, not a universal mechanism claim.
(ix)~The dev-fitted affine control sits at its coefficient grid edge for
one seed (s11); affine conclusions are comparisons, not exact fits.
(x)~The confirmation bank has been reused across analyses (P1 first,
relational-arm evaluation later, relabeling robustness last); usage is
documented per claim (Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}), and first-read status is
claimed only where it applies.
(xi)~Every absolute rate is reported under one labeling convention of the
four input points. The oracle is invariant under the task's relabeling
symmetry but the raw-coordinate models are not: on the dev bank the
four-arm joint consistency of raw arms swings by $75$--$133\%$ of its mean
across the eight relabelings ($15$--$29\%$ for relational input with flip
coverage), and $42$--$48\%$ of quartets change their joint-correctness
status. Our headline levels are therefore convention-relative; the
comparisons that drive the paper's conclusions are the ones this sweep
reproduces in all $24$ seed$\times$relabeling cells on both banks.
A later matched-recipe check on this same dev bank removes the swing by
building the symmetry into the features, and raises joint correctness;
augmentation alone does not remove the swing (App.~\ref{sec:l015}).

\paragraph{Scope of causal explanations.}
Fixed-recipe negatives do not exclude capacity or optimization. Ordering
is $S(f_{\mathrm{head}}\circ h)$, not an encoder-only property; oracle-selected
$J^*$ is a diagnostic bound, not deployable accuracy. Corrected continuation,
football construction, and policy analyses are reported separately in
App.~\ref{sec:f095}; historical loss, infeasibility, and calibration-exclusion
claims are withdrawn.
