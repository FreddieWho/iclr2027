\section{Related Work and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Related work.} Intervention-based evaluation has lineage in causal
abstractions and interchange analysis (activation swaps for causal-model
alignment); ours differs in using input-space reallocation and semantic
composition as the prediction target. Sensitivity analysis (Morris elementary
effects; budgeted perturbations) supplies the screening metaphor, but our
screen is semantic: edits with known oracle outcomes, not random directions.
Compositional generalization benchmarks test unseen combinations at the
\emph{data} level; we test unseen joint outcomes at the \emph{decision-update}
level, conditioned on correct atomic judgments---a strictly harder bar that
static-accuracy evaluations miss by construction. Prior probing literature
(Jacobian geometry, spectral readouts, equivariance measurement) motivates our
diagnostic instruments but is background, not claim: an earlier line of this
project on support-reallocation probing and Jacobian-Gram prediction is
retained only as methodological context, superseded by the update-failure
results above.

\paragraph{Limitations (all evidenced, none hidden).}
(i)~Single-frame snapshots; no temporal modeling.
(ii)~One coordinate encoder family plus one small CNN pixel family;
architecture generality untested.
(iii)~Synthetic coordinate scenes are not real tactical changes; distance to
tactical semantics unmeasured.
(iv)~Football evidence is three-seed same-direction without a pristine
holdout; the single-read proof lives on the coordinate side.
(v)~Pixel cross-style repair rests on three training seeds, all same-direction $5/5$;
absolute pixel levels are style-sensitive (color-swap collapse reported).
(vi)~The X03 negative is scoped to one layer and one rank-4 patch form.
(vii)~Small-effect magnitudes on real data are reported without inflation;
the weakest seed stays in every table.
