\section{Related Work and Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Related work.} Intervention-based evaluation has lineage in causal
abstractions and interchange analysis (activation swaps for causal-model
alignment; \citep{geiger2021causal}); ours differs in using input-space reallocation and semantic
composition as the prediction target. Sensitivity analysis (Morris elementary
effects; \citep{morris1991factorial}) supplies the screening metaphor, but our
screen is semantic: edits with known oracle outcomes, not random directions.
On compositional generalization we do not originate conditional-on-atomics
measurement: the compositionality gap (correct sub-problems, failed
composition) is established for language models
\citep{press2023measuring}, with a
stricter double-gated variant for factual composition
\citep{yu2026compositioncollapse}. Our differences: (1)~controlled semantic change with known
boundaries; (2)~same-instance transition structure (paths, not just
endpoints); (3)~confidence meaning reversal under semantic flips;
(4)~repair error migration inside the same quartet; (5)~downstream
consequence. On calibration and selective classification
\citep{guo2017calibration,geifman2017selective} our contrast is explicit: both concern
\emph{current} correctness, while our inversion concerns reliability
\emph{after} a true semantic change. Excessive invariance (adversarial
vulnerability from over-robust features; \citep{jacobsen2019excessive}) is adjacent in flavor but distinct
in object: fixed-decision robustness versus failure to change decisions.
Counterfactual repair and positive-congruent (regression-free) updates
\citep{kaushik2020learning,yan2021positive} are the right neighborhoods for our flip coverage;
we claim no priority on preserve constraints, only the instance-level
migration measurement and the coverage/quality decomposition. Prior probing
literature (Jacobian geometry, spectral readouts, equivariance measurement;
\citep{belinkov2022probing,lenc2015understanding})
motivates our diagnostic instruments but is background, not claim.

\paragraph{Limitations (all evidenced, none hidden).}
(i)~P1 strong evidence is currently coordinate-only; natural football turns
show floor/null for confidence inversion.
(ii)~Models remain small task-trained models plus one small CNN pixel
family; the foundation bridge never established atomic competence, so
architecture generality is untested.
(iii)~Synthetic coordinate scenes are not real tactical changes; distance to
tactical semantics unmeasured.
(iv)~Football evidence is three-seed same-direction without a pristine
holdout; the single-read proof lives on the coordinate side.
(v)~Pixel cross-style repair rests on three training seeds, all same-direction $5/5$;
absolute pixel levels are style-sensitive (color-swap collapse reported).
(vi)~The X03 negative is scoped to one layer and one rank-4 patch form.
(vii)~P3 joint-consistency absolute values are low (single-digit to
$\sim$0.45 even with relational features); small-effect magnitudes on real
data are reported without inflation; the weakest seed stays in every table.
(viii)~Relational features are hand-designed and relation-aware by
construction; no universal mechanism is claimed.
(ix)~The dev-fitted affine control used against flip coverage sits at its
coefficient grid edge for one seed (s11), so that control is read as bounded;
all affine conclusions are stated as comparisons, not exact fits.
