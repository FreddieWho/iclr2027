\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Static correctness is not reliable updating. This paper asks when
supervision of a single change improves full compositional correctness,
rather than only moving the error, and what role input representation and
cross-object interaction play.

An endpoint rate does not answer it. On oracle-known edits, both atomic
edits can be judged correctly while the joint change is still missed in
$151/176 = 85.8\%$ of fresh-holdout cases (Sec.~\ref{sec:phenomenon}).
On the historical development bank, raw flip coverage mostly migrates that
error on the baseline-broken subset (full repair $0.03$--$0.10$, migration
$0.36$--$0.40$; Sec.~\ref{sec:repair}). That is not every repair. A
ResNet-18 on a separate rendered bank is a boundary: full repair exceeds
migration on every fixed baseline-110 subset (Sec.~\ref{sec:pixel}).
Matched total exposure is not matched per-parent exposure.

The comparison in hand is an input comparison, not a new architecture.
Relational features with the same flip coverage move joint consistency
toward full repair. A fresh-source analytic segment-intersection parser
solves its E bank, so these learned source comparisons are parser-bounded
diagnostics rather than evidence that a complex relation architecture is
required. The $237$- and $509$-quartet banks stay separate. Input
advantage transfers to point-in-triangle and segment--disk; positive
interaction does not, and the segment--disk joint-scale interaction stays
negative. The source-task typed negative does not exclude learnable
relational ability: segment-sum plus a linear readout cannot represent a
general crossing (Sec.~\ref{sec:repair}). A repaired model is not claimed
to succeed.

Coordinate confidence is a scoped side result. It ranks current-state error
classically and update error in reverse on coordinate scenes, and the
reversal is not reproduced on natural football turns
(Sec.~\ref{sec:phenomenon}).

\paragraph{Contributions.} (C1)~Paired full-repair, migration, and joint
consistency measurement, with historical banks on their own denominators.
(C2)~An input comparison that separates input advantage from positive
interaction transfer, with a parser-bounded source diagnostic rather than a
new-architecture claim. (C3)~The same accounting on a competent ResNet-18,
where full repair exceeds migration. Confidence inversion is not the center.
