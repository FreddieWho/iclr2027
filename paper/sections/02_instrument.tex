\section{Controlled Changes and Evaluation}
\label{sec:setting}

\paragraph{Scenes, edits, and the test population.} A base scene $x$ has
oracle label $y(x) \in \{0,1\}$ with margin $m(x)$. An atomic edit $e$
maps $x \mapsto x+e$ with known oracle outcome; a composed edit $e_a+e_b$
has a known \emph{joint} outcome that may differ from either atomic
outcome. We write $x_A = x + e_A$, $x_B = x + e_B$, $x_{AB} = x + e_A +
e_B$, and study the test population of atomic-preserving, joint-flipping
quartets
\[
E \;=\; \{\, (x, e_A, e_B) \;:\; y(x_A) = y(x_B) = y(x), \;\; y(x_{AB})
\neq y(x) \,\}.
\]
On $E$, the conditional composition miss (CCM) is the error of the joint
prediction given both atomic predictions correct. CCM always means a miss.
Figure~\ref{fig:repair_examples} shows two archived examples selected by
the fixed rule in \texttt{reports/e832\_focus/FIGURE1\_SELECTION\_RECEIPT.json};
they illustrate repair flow and are not used to estimate an effect.
Joint consistency
$J = P(\hat y(x_A) = y(x_A),\; \hat y(x_B) = y(x_B),\; \hat y(x_{AB}) =
y(x_{AB}) \mid E)$
and atomic-joint accuracy are conditional joint accuracy. Neither is
abbreviated CCM, and a rising conditional joint success rate is not a CCM. For repair evaluation we fix the baseline-broken subset
$H = \{ \hat y_{\mathrm{clean}}(x_A) = y(x_A),\; \hat y_{\mathrm{clean}}(x_B)
= y(x_B),\; \hat y_{\mathrm{clean}}(x_{AB}) \neq y(x_{AB}) \}$ (both
atomics correct, AB wrong under the clean model) and decompose endpoint
repair by the identity
\[
R_{\mathrm{endpoint}} \;=\; R_{\mathrm{full}} + M,
\]
where $R_{\mathrm{full}}$ is the fraction of $H$ made fully correct
($111$) and $M$ (error migration) is the fraction whose AB endpoint flips
correct while at least one atomic becomes wrong. We also report whether the
base state $x$ itself is correctly classified as an audit column; headline
denominators ($151/176$) are unchanged by this audit.

\paragraph{What ``updating'' means here.} All models in this study are
feed-forward classifiers that re-compute a decision independently for each
state; none carries hidden state across edits. ``Updating'' is therefore a
\emph{behavioral} test on independently computed decisions, not a claim
about internal memory, recurrence, or world models. The relation between a
model's response to a mid-path state and its endpoint classification error
is exactly the object we measure, not an assumed mechanism.

\paragraph{Models and data.} Table~\ref{tab:setup} summarizes the four
observation domains. \emph{Coordinate scenes} are synthetic two-segment
line-crossing tasks (four points, eight input coordinates). Development
uses fixed pools (\texttt{train\_101}, \texttt{eval\_202}); all headline
discovery numbers are re-tested on a freshly minted, pre-registered holdout
(\texttt{holdout\_909}), evaluated once in a single uninterrupted run and
then archived as used. Mechanism analyses read frozen sample banks
(\texttt{bank\_dev512} of 237 quartets, plus parent-disjoint
\texttt{bank\_confirm1007} of 509 quartets) in which every model reads
identical edits; thresholds and analysis settings are frozen on dev before
confirmation. These are separate banks and are not pooled.
\texttt{bank\_dev512} is a repeatedly analyzed historical bank, not a
sealed confirmation bank. This paper does not repeat an integer-ID leakage
charge against it. Seeds are training replicates, not datasets. \emph{Football} uses IDSSE real tracking data with two edit
types, labeled explicitly throughout: controlled single-turn zone
crossings constructed on real coordinates (E2 protocol), and natural-match
turns (actual events). There is no pristine football holdout (the reserve
field saw development use), so football evidence is reported as
three-seed same-direction confirmation with cross-field evaluation.
\emph{Pixel observations} render the same quartet geometry as $64\times64$
images judged by a small CNN trained from scratch per arm.

\paragraph{Reporting convention (stated once).} Every rate in this paper
carries its denominator and its conditioning. Discovery numbers and fresh
confirmation numbers are reported side by side, labeled, and never pooled;
matched-single controls use different denominators than conditional
composition measures and are quoted as descriptive contrasts only.
Confidence-quartile curves for current-state error are computed over
unscreened starts; update-error curves are additionally conditioned on
start correctness. Bootstrap intervals cluster by parent (coordinate side)
or match (football side); a start appearing in several quartets is not
treated as independent evidence.
