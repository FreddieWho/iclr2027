# Research: S1 big-number hunting (dev-only, no new data)

## Summary
Most headroom is in **margins (0.01–0.17 vs 0.64 frozen)** — a 4–60× gap to an internal reference. Second is **pair (+0.0092) / zone (+0.0054)**, small absolute values where 2–3× relative gains stay plausible. **Intervention (0.81–0.99 dev) and T5R6 8/8 external are saturated** — poor headline targets. The three dev-only plays below are ranked by expected headline size per cost.

## Findings

### Play 1 — Margin–SupCon hybrid + temperature sweep → target: margin metric
1. **Claim:** Thin margins with a frozen reference at 0.64 is the largest explicit headroom; angular-margin and SupCon families are the directly relevant lever. **Sources:** Deng et al., ArcFace (CVPR 2019) (https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.07698); Khosla et al., Supervised Contrastive Learning (NeurIPS 2020) (https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11362). **Support:** direct evidence (ArcFace "significantly enhances discriminative power" via additive angular margin; SupCon 81.4% top-1 on ResNet-200 ImageNet, +0.8pp over best reported). **Confidence:** medium.
- **Experiment (dev-only):** freeze encoder, sweep SupCon temperature τ {0.05,0.07,0.1,0.2} × margin-hybrid weight (e.g., SupCon + triplet/ArcFace-style term λ {0.2,0.5,1.0}); Sub-center variant K=3 if noisy positives suspected (Sub-center ArcFace, ECCV 2020, https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/blob/master/recognition/subcenter_arcface/README.md — K=3 "good balance between accuracy and robustness"). Report margin + pair/zone jointly to catch tradeoffs.
- **Expected range (researcher inference, low confidence):** margin 0.01–0.17 → 0.25–0.45 (still below 0.64 frozen); pair/zone may move ±50% as side effect.
- **Cost:** low (loss-only change, short retrains + eval). **Risk:** medium — contrastive objectives compress intra-class variance, which can *hurt* fine-grained r@1; one comparison found triplet beat contrastive on r@1 (CIFAR-10 0.919 vs 0.843; Cars 0.298 vs 0.254) (arXiv 2510.02161, https://arxiv.org/html/2510.02161v2). Mitigate by reporting both margin and retrieval metrics.
- **Evidence grade:** B (primary venues, but transfer to your geometry is interpretation).

### Play 2 — Long schedule for seed-23 triplet (unconverged) → target: pair / zone
1. **Claim:** Contrastive/metric losses improve with much longer training than supervised CE; an unconverged seed is the cheapest credible big-number bet. **Sources:** Chen et al., SimCLR (ICML 2020) via Google Research summary (https://research.google/blog/advancing-self-supervised-and-semi-supervised-learning-with-simclr); Lightly SimCLR docs (https://docs.lightly.ai/train/stable/methods/simclr.html). **Support:** direct evidence ("supervised ResNet peaked between 90–300 epochs, but SimCLR can continue improvement even after 800 epochs"; "top-1 continues to increase even after 3200 epochs"). **Confidence:** medium for direction, low for magnitude.
- **Experiment:** 2–3× schedule on seed-23 triplet only (same data, cosine/long-warmup schedule, lower final LR); track loss curve to prove convergence; also try Ranked-List-style use of more negatives per batch (Wang et al., arXiv 1903.03238, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.03238) as a schedule-adjacent variant.
- **Expected range (researcher inference, low confidence):** pair +0.0092 → +0.015–0.030; zone +0.0054 → +0.008–0.015; margin +0.02–0.10. Gains are logarithmic — headline is relative (+100–200%), not absolute.
- **Cost:** medium (one long run + evals). **Risk:** low technically, medium narratively (reviewers discount "just trained longer" unless framed as convergence correction for seed-23).
- **Evidence grade:** B+ for direction (strong primary literature), C for exact range (inference).

### Play 3 — Attention/GeM readout (+ small capacity bump) → target: centroid −34% / geometry-ρ 0.5–0.85
1. **Claim:** Pooling/readout changes give retrieval gains independent of loss/schedule; GeM explicitly generalizes mean/max pooling. **Sources:** Radenović et al., Revisiting Oxford and Paris (CVPR 2018) (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/324150876_Revisiting_Oxford_and_Paris_Large-Scale_Image_Retrieval_Benchmarking); blog exposition of GeM (https://amaarora.github.io/posts/2020-08-30-gempool.html); attention-pooling as learned alternative to mean (https://ai-solutions.daviesmeyer.com/en/glossary/attention-pooling). **Support:** direct evidence (GeM "generalizes max and average pooling… boosts retrieval"; pk→∞ = max, pk=1 = average). **Confidence:** medium-low for transfer.
- **Experiment:** swap mean readout → learnable GeM (p init ~3) or single-head attention pooling; optionally +1 transformer layer / wider projection (capacity bump) with all else fixed. Report centroid shift, ρ, *and* frozen-reference margin to prove no collapse.
- **Expected range (researcher inference, low confidence):** centroid −34% → −40 to −48%; ρ 0.5–0.85 → upper end 0.88–0.93. Single-digit-pp moves, but reads as headline when paired with Play 1 margins.
- **Cost:** medium-high (arch change, retrain, ablate p/head). **Risk:** high — most likely to overfit dev or break frozen comparability; attention readouts can memorize dev geometry. Gate on T5R6-style external check before claiming.
- **Evidence grade:** C+ (strong retrieval precedent, weakest transfer to your centroid/ρ).

## What NOT to hunt
- **Intervention 0.81–0.99 dev:** ceiling-bound; any gain is <0.2 and invites "saturated metric" criticism. *Researcher inference.*
- **T5R6 8/8 external:** perfect score has zero upward headroom; use only as guardrail. *Researcher inference.*

## Contradictions
- SupCon/contrastive superiority (Khosla NeurIPS 2020) vs triplet-superior r@1 in variance analysis (arXiv 2510.02161): triplet's broader intra-class variance preserves fine distinctions. Implication: Play 1 hybrids must be checked against pair/zone, not margin alone.
- Longer-training optimism (SimCLR literature) vs margin-scheduler caution (arXiv 2603.26389, https://arxiv.org/html/2603.26389v1): fixed margins go stale as embeddings evolve; naive long runs without margin/temperature maintenance may plateau. Favors combining Plays 1+2.

## Missing evidence
- No verified learning-curve data for seed-23 (how far from convergence); Play 2 range is extrapolated from vision contrastive literature, not your logs.
- No baseline for current readout (mean vs GeM gap unknown); Play 3 could be ±zero.
- Margin definition vs ArcFace angular margin not confirmed commensurable with 0.64 frozen reference — Play 1's "gap" assumes comparability.
