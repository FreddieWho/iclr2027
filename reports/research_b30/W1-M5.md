# Research: M5 — variance decomposition design for small-seed DL experiments

## Summary
With only 3 seeds (11/23/47), stable metrics (zone F1) support descriptive consistency claims, but volatile metrics (pair ranking 0.52–0.88, intervention ρ 0.29–0.99) cannot support winner or effect-size claims. Report all 3 per-seed values with min–max ranges (not ±std, not bootstrap-over-3-seeds), use bootstrap-over-data only for sampling uncertainty, and gate strong claims to ≥10 seeds per Colas/Agarwal logic and the NeurIPS/ICLR checklist (Q7 statistical significance).

## Findings

**1. Claim: 3 seeds suffice only for narrow-spread descriptive claims; comparative/volatile claims need ~5–10+ seeds.**
**Sources:** Colas et al. 2018 (arXiv:1806.08295); Henderson et al., AAAI 2018; Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021 (Outstanding Paper). **Support:** interpretation grounded in direct evidence. Colas et al. explicitly relate seed count to Type-I/II error rates for t-tests and bootstrap CIs, showing splits of 5 seeds can look like different distributions and that nearly all reviewed papers used ≤5 seeds — unreliably. Henderson et al. demonstrate algorithm rankings flip under seed variation and advocate significance testing. Agarwal et al. validate stratified-bootstrap CI coverage as a function of run count (3/5/10/20/…) — intervals with 3 runs are very wide by construction. Researcher inference: our pair-ranking spread (0.52–0.88) and intervention-ρ spread (0.29–0.99) are exactly the flip regime these papers warn about; zone F1, if its 3-seed range is tight, is the only claim class 3 seeds can carry. **Confidence:** high for the qualitative tiering; medium for any exact "10 seeds" threshold (power depends on effect size; Colas's tables are RL-specific).

**2. Claim: bootstrap-over-seeds with n=3 is degenerate; bootstrap-over-data answers a different question — report both denominators separately.**
**Sources:** Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021; Dodge et al., EMNLP 2019 ("Show Your Work," ACL Anthology D19-1224); Bouthillier et al., ICML 2019 (PMLR v97). **Support:** interpretation. Bootstrap-over-seeds estimates training stochasticity (init, shuffling); with n=3 the empirical distribution has 2 d.f. and resampling it yields unstable/overconfident intervals (Colas notes bootstrap CIs need more seeds than t-tests). Bootstrap-over-data (resampling zones/pairs/patients within a fixed trained model) estimates evaluation-sampling noise conditional on that seed — feasible even with 3 seeds. Bouthillier et al. decompose total variance into initialization, data-order, and data-sampling sources via the law of total variance, showing fixing any one source limits generality. Researcher inference: recommended design is nested — per-seed value + within-seed data-bootstrap CI, then raw seed spread across the 3 — never a single pooled number. A future variance-decomposition needs a seeds×resamples grid, not computable from 3 seeds alone. **Confidence:** high (conceptual); medium on quantitative decomposition without new runs.

**3. Claim: NeurIPS/ICLR checklist Q7 requires defined error bars for main claims — denominator, sigma level, and compute — and forbids max-across-seeds reporting.**
**Sources:** NeurIPS Paper Checklist Guide (neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist; 2022 checklist wording); ACL Rolling Review Responsible NLP Research checklist (built on Dodge et al. 2019). **Support:** direct evidence. Q7 asks "Does the paper report error bars suitably and correctly defined or other appropriate information about the statistical significance of the experiments?" — Yes requires error bars/CIs/tests for main experiments; guidance states: declare 1-σ vs 2-σ, prefer 2-σ over Normality-assumed 95% CIs, avoid symmetric bars for asymmetric/bounded metrics (e.g., correlations bounded in [−1,1]), and report compute resources. Dodge et al. require stating whether a number is single-run, max-across-N, or mean-over-N, and propose expected-validation-performance-vs-budget curves. Picard (arXiv:2109.08203) shows seed choice alone moves accuracy substantially — the "lucky seed" mechanism. **Confidence:** high for checklist content; medium that ICLR 2027 wording matches NeurIPS verbatim (ICLR has historically mirrored it; verify at submission).

## Prescriptions for OUR reporting (≤3)

**P1 — Claim-tier gating by observed spread.** Tier-S (stable, e.g., zone F1 *iff* 3-seed range stays narrow): allowed claim is "consistent across 3 seeds," shown as all three values + range; no p-values, no winner language. Tier-V (volatile: pair ranking 0.52–0.88; intervention ρ 0.29–0.99): label exploratory/seed-sensitive; forbid "method A beats B" and forbid point-estimate leaderboards; confirmatory comparison requires a pre-registered expansion to ≥10 seeds with CIs (Colas/Agarwal basis). Evidence grade: B (strong literature, thresholds extrapolated to our metrics).

**P2 — Honest spread presentation.** Per config, table columns: seed 11 / 23 / 47 / min–max (never mean±std alone at n=3; never symmetric bars on ρ/ranking). Optionally add within-seed bootstrap-over-data CIs for ranking/ρ to show sampling noise ≠ seed noise. Caption must state "spread over n=3 training seeds, descriptive only; bootstrap (if shown) is over evaluation units within seed." Never report best-of-3 as the headline (Dodge). Evidence grade: A (checklist + Dodge direct).

**P3 — Checklist-ready provenance.** For each main figure state: error-bar denominator (seeds vs data), σ-level, run count, seeds fixed in advance (11/23/47), and compute; cite key references: Henderson et al., AAAI 2018; Colas et al. 2018; Dodge et al., EMNLP 2019; Bouthillier et al., ICML 2019; Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021. Evidence grade: A (venue/year verified via sources above).

## Contradictions
None found on core guidance. One tension recorded: small-sample ML folklore sometimes treats mean±std over 3 seeds as sufficient for publication; the cited literature (Colas, Agarwal, Henderson) explicitly rejects this for comparative claims — resolved in favor of the literature by tiering claims rather than inflating n=3 statistics.

## Missing evidence
Exact seed-count thresholds for *our* metrics (zone F1 vs ranking vs ρ) need a power calculation or a 10+-seed pilot — cannot be derived from n=3. Within-seed data-bootstrap CIs not yet computed. ICLR 2027 checklist wording not re-verified at submission time.
