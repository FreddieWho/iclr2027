# Research: S4 Related-Work Judo — 4 Differentiator + Conversion Pairs

## Summary
Each of the four nearest neighbors is conceded its core strength, then reframed as a premise our work builds on: Gruver measures equivariance post-hoc (we intervene on it); Geiger abstracts semantics (we abstract symmetry); TacticAI hard-codes one symmetry for one sport (we generalize the recipe); Partial G-CNNs learn relaxation inside the model (we supply the diagnostic of where/when to relax).

## Findings

### 1. Gruver et al., ICLR 2023 — descriptive measurement vs. interventional equivariance
- **Exact citation:** Gruver, Finzi, Goldblum & Wilson, "The Lie Derivative for Measuring Learned Equivariance," Proc. ICLR 2023 (arXiv:2210.02984). **Sources:** [ICLR poster page](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2023/poster/11044), [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02984).
- **One-sentence differentiator:** Gruver et al. use the Lie derivative to *measure how much equivariance hundreds of pretrained CNNs/transformers/Mixers happened to learn*; we test equivariance *causally*, by intervening on group actions and checking behavioral consequences, not infinitesimal generator alignment.
- **Conversion sentence (their strength → our asset):** "Their large-scale result — that architecture alone does not determine learned equivariance and that training factors dominate — is exactly why a post-hoc metric is insufficient and an interventional framework like ours is needed: we turn their descriptive finding into a prescriptive test."
- **Support:** direct evidence (their abstract states the scale/architecture-vs-training separation claim). **Confidence:** high. **Evidence grade: A** (primary venue page + arXiv record agree).

### 2. Geiger et al., NeurIPS 2021 — semantic abstraction vs. symmetry abstraction
- **Exact citation:** Geiger, Potts, Yu, Icard et al., "Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks," Proc. NeurIPS 2021 (arXiv:2106.02997). **Sources:** [NeurIPS proceedings page](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/4f5c422f4d49a5a807eda27434231040-Abstract.html), [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997).
- **One-sentence differentiator:** Geiger et al. align neural representations to variables of an interpretable *semantic* causal model and verify via interchange interventions; we align network behavior to a *symmetry* causal model (group actions as the high-level variables), so equivariance itself becomes the abstraction under test.
- **Conversion sentence:** "Their interchange-intervention machinery is the gold standard for 'does the network really implement this computation' — we inherit that legitimacy wholesale and instantiate it for group structure, so reviewers who accept causal abstraction must accept our evaluation protocol as a special case."
- **Support:** direct evidence for their method definition (proceedings abstract); interpretation for the "special case" framing (researcher inference — Geiger never discusses group actions). **Confidence:** medium-high. **Evidence grade: A−** (method description verified; framing move is ours and must be labeled as such).

### 3. TacticAI (Wang et al.), Nature Communications 2024 — one fixed symmetry vs. general recipe
- **Exact citation:** Wang, Devlin, Papalampidi et al. (Google DeepMind + Liverpool FC), "TacticAI: an AI assistant for football tactics," Nature Communications 15 (2024), Article s41467-024-45965-x (preprint arXiv:2310.10553, Oct 2023). **Sources:** [Nature page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-45965-x), [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10553).
- **One-sentence differentiator:** TacticAI bakes a single fixed symmetry (D2-equivariant GNN over player graphs) into one domain (corner kicks) to maximize task performance; we do not compete on corner-kick accuracy but provide the general framework that decides *which* symmetry to bake in, for *which* components, on *any* multi-agent data.
- **Conversion sentence:** "Their state-of-the-art results plus expert evaluation with Liverpool FC prove our motivating premise — symmetry-aware GNNs beat generic models on sport data — and their single-symmetry design choice is precisely the point of failure our framework diagnoses and generalizes."
- **Support:** direct evidence (Nature abstract: geometric-DL pipeline, corner-kick focus, Liverpool FC collaboration, SOTA claims); interpretation for "D2 fixed" detail (widely reported in paper body; arXiv landing page confirms GNN pipeline but D2 specifics live in full text — researcher has not re-fetched full text here). **Confidence:** medium-high. **Evidence grade: B+** (venue/year/domain verified; cite the full-text D2 claim only after re-checking the paper PDF before camera-ready).

### 4. Romero & Bekkers et al., NeurIPS 2022 (Partial G-CNNs) — learning relaxation vs. diagnosing it
- **Exact citation:** Romero, Bekkers, Tomczak & Welling, "Learning Partial Equivariances from Data," Proc. NeurIPS 2022 (arXiv:2110.10211). **Sources:** [NeurIPS proceedings page](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/ec51d1fe4bbb754577da5e18eb54e6d1-Abstract-Conference.html), [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.10211v2).
- **One-sentence differentiator:** Partial G-CNNs *learn layer-wise levels* of partial/full equivariance to discrete, continuous, or combined groups end-to-end inside the architecture; we operate one level up, *measuring and attributing* where full equivariance holds, breaks, or hurts, independent of any single architecture family.
- **Conversion sentence:** "Their headline result — that the optimal equivariance level differs per layer and full equivariance is often suboptimal — is our paper's founding assumption; they supply the mechanism that exploits partial equivariance, we supply the diagnostic that tells practitioners when and where to use it."
- **Support:** direct evidence (proceedings abstract states layer-wise partial/full equivariance learned in training verbatim). **Confidence:** high. **Evidence grade: A** (claim quotes the official abstract).
