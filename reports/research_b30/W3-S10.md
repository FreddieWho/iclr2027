# Research: S10 — Multi-Agent Representation Probing Benchmark

## Summary
Benchmarks that endure (BEIR, VTAB, OGB) share: heterogeneous/diverse tasks, a fixed thin evaluation interface (standard format, light head, unified evaluator), and funded maintenance with versioning. From our artifacts the minimal viable benchmark is a **probe-discipline audit**: frozen representations + locks/single-read enforcement + control-task selectivity + support-reallocation and Slepian/mu checks. Maintenance is the binding constraint — leaderboards, contamination control, and dataset fixes (cf. OGB's code-bug fix) cost far more than the initial release.

## Findings

1. **Claim:** BEIR succeeded via heterogeneity + zero-shot protocol + standardized format and runnable code examples, and showed consistent cross-dataset performance is hard. **Sources:** Thakur et al., BEIR (NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks, 2021). **Support:** direct evidence (abstract/paper pages state heterogeneous zero-shot benchmark, standardized format, code examples; finding that models struggle consistently). **Confidence:** high.
2. **Claim:** VTAB's design principle — good representations adapt to diverse unseen tasks with few examples using a lightweight head — is the closest template for probing; it separates representation quality from adaptation machinery. **Sources:** Zhai et al., VTAB (arXiv 1910.04867, 2019; Google Research release 2019). **Support:** direct evidence for principle and diversity/few-example framing. **Confidence:** high.
3. **Claim:** OGB's lesson is realism + scale + tooling (auto-download data loader, unified evaluator, splits) plus active versioned maintenance; even OGB shipped a dataset bug requiring a fix, and ran costly leaderboard/challenge tracks (LSC @ NeurIPS 2022). **Sources:** Hu et al., OGB (NeurIPS 2020); OGB site/leaderboard pages. **Support:** direct evidence for loader/evaluator/leaderboard/versioning; interpretation that maintenance cost dominates. **Confidence:** high for tooling/versioning; medium for cost magnitude (researcher inference from LSC compute columns, e.g., 8×V100/A100 submissions).
4. **Claim:** Probing methodology requires control tasks/selectivity: a probe should score high on the linguistic task and low on a random-output control learnable only by memorization; many popular probes fail this. Foundational move is frozen-representation + linear classifier probes. **Sources:** Hewitt & Liang (EMNLP 2019); Alain & Bengio (arXiv 2016). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.
5. **Claim:** Benchmarks saturate and contaminate (GLUE→SuperGLUE cycle; researcher inference applied to probing: without control tasks and held-out probes, a probing benchmark will be gamed by expressive probes memorizing). Recent leakage-controlled probing benchmarks (e.g., CodonBench, bioRxiv Aug 2026, thin-signal audit cascade) show the community now expects leakage accounting as a first-class metric. **Sources:** CodonBench preprint page (bioRxiv 2026); GLUE/SuperGLUE history (background knowledge). **Support:** direct evidence for CodonBench leakage focus; interpretation for saturation claim. **Confidence:** medium.
6. **Claim:** Cost/maintenance reality: a live leaderboard needs versioning, submission validation/reproducibility checks, contamination monitoring, and periodic task refresh — OGB explicitly polices invalid submissions and versions datasets. An unmaintained benchmark decays into a static dataset within ~1–2 years. **Sources:** OGB leaderboard policy pages. **Support:** interpretation (researcher inference from stated policies + LSC scale). **Confidence:** medium.

## Three benchmark formulations (max 3)

**B1 — Probe-Discipline Audit [RECOMMENDED MVP] (evidence grade: A — directly from our artifacts).**
Scope: single frozen multi-agent representation set + fixed probe family (linear, fixed rank/budget). Tasks: (i) target-attribute probe accuracy; (ii) matched control-task accuracy → selectivity gap; (iii) lock/single-read violation test (any second read or unlock = fail); (iv) support-reallocation robustness (score delta under operator). Metrics: selectivity = acc_ling − acc_control; leakage delta; pass/fail discipline gates; mean + CI over seeds. Release: v0 static split + evaluator script + 1-page discipline spec; no leaderboard (result table in repo). Cost: lowest; maintenance ~versioned splits only.

**B2 — Slepian + μ-Covariate Generalization Suite (grade: B — needs new held-out environments).**
Scope: B1 plus localized Slepian probe tasks and μ-shifted test environments. Tasks: band-limited probe recovery at held-out positions/agents; μ-stratified evaluation (in-μ vs out-μ). Metrics: recovery error vs concentration bound; out-μ degradation slope; calibration of μ-conditioned predictions. Release: v0 with 2–3 environments, evaluator enforcing frozen-probe rule; leaderboard optional/frozen after workshop. Cost: medium (new data curation); risk: Slepian construction choices become the benchmark (must freeze and version them).

**B3 — Cross-Agent Transfer Challenge (grade: C — wild/community, highest cost).**
Scope: train probes on agent-set A, evaluate zero-shot on agent-set B/C (BEIR-style zero-shot transfer). Tasks: heterogeneous probe tasks across agent architectures/seeds. Metrics: mean + worst-task score (BEIR lesson: consistency is the signal), selectivity-adjusted. Release: competition track with hidden test probes, submission code requirement, annual refresh. Cost: highest; needs funding, contamination policing, challenge infra (OGB-LSC analog).

## Maintenance risk (explicit)
- **High risk for B3:** leaderboard gaming, test-probe leakage into training corpora, submission validation load; requires named maintainers + compute/time budget or it should not be promised.
- **Medium risk for B2:** Slepian/μ design drift and environment bugs (cf. OGB code-bug precedent); mitigate by frozen v0 + changelog + version-gated results.
- **Low risk for B1:** static audit + script; still needs contact owner and deprecation policy. Recommendation: ship B1 as the paper contribution; frame B2 as pilot; offer B3 only as future-work contingent on maintainers.
- Staleness flag: CodonBench-style leakage accounting is 2026-fresh; GLUE-era saturation dynamics are stable background knowledge, not fresh evidence.

## Contradictions
None found among primary sources on core design lessons. Tension noted: VTAB-style light-head evaluation vs. probing-selectivity literature — a light head alone does not guarantee selectivity (Hewitt & Liang show expressive-but-thin probes still memorize); researcher inference: benchmark must cap probe capacity AND require control tasks.
