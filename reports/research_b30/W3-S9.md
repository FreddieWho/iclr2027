# Research S9: Neuroscience connections — grid/place cells and position–context separation

## Summary
The brain does separate "where" from "what," but not cleanly: medial entorhinal cortex (MEC) carries space/structure, lateral entorhinal cortex (LEC) carries objects/items, and hippocampus binds them. The strongest analogy to a repair separating absolute position (context) from internal shape (intrinsic) is the **structure-vs-sensory factorization** in the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine; the classic LEC/MEC what-vs-where split is a medium analogy weakened by mixed coding; grid-as-metric is the weakest and mostly decorative. Decoration risk is high unless the brief states a falsifiable mapping.

## Findings

**1. Rank 1 (most substantive): structure–sensory factorization (Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine).**
- **Claim:** MEC encodes reusable abstract structure (transition/graph relations); hippocampus binds that structure to current sensory observations; LEC supplies the sensory/item side. This maps directly: intrinsic shape → structural code (position-free relations); absolute position → sensory/context observation bound at retrieval. **Sources:** Whittington et al., Cell (2020); Connor & Knierim, Nature Reviews Neuroscience (2017). **Support:** direct evidence (TEM abstract explicitly states "medial entorhinal cells form a basis describing structural knowledge, and hippocampal cells link this basis with sensory representations"; TEM reproduces grid/border/object-vector cells from this factorization). **Confidence:** medium-high for the analogy logic; medium for neuroscience ground truth.
- **What makes it more than decoration:** state a test the repair must pass, e.g. shape code learned in one absolute-position regime must transfer zero-shot to a new regime (like TEM generalizing structure to new sensory environments); failure kills the analogy. Report transfer gap numerically.
- Researcher inference: this is the only formulation where both sides have matching factorization semantics (relations vs. observations), not just shared vocabulary.

**2. Rank 2: LEC (what/object) vs MEC (where/space) dual streams converging on place cells.**
- **Claim:** Place cells in hippocampus (O'Keefe & Dostrovsky, 1971; Nobel 2014 shared with the Mosers) gain spatial selectivity mainly from MEC and item/context modulation mainly from LEC; anatomy supports dorsal-"where" vs ventral-"what" streams. **Sources:** Knierim, Neunuebel & Deshmukh, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B (2014); Nobel Prize press/advanced information (2014); Hafting et al. (Moser lab), Nature (2005) for grid cells. **Support:** interpretation — the anatomical framing is real but the clean split is contested. **Confidence:** medium for anatomy; low that the split is clean.
- **What maps:** absolute position → MEC/path-integration input; internal shape identity → LEC object/context input; repaired representation → hippocampal conjunction. **What makes it more than decoration:** show double dissociation in the repair: ablating position-context and shape-intrinsic channels degrades different error types, mirroring MEC vs LEC lesion logic. Without that experiment it is vocabulary borrowing.
- Caveat (direct evidence): mixed object+spatial coding exists in both MEC and LEC (Keene et al., J. Neuroscience 2016; Fernández-Ruiz review, 2016 — "simple framework challenged by mixed coding"). So do not cite the what-vs-where split as fact; cite it as tendency. **Confidence in caveat:** high.

**3. Rank 3 (weakest, decoration-leaning): grid-cell continuous-attractor metric as "intrinsic coordinate frame."**
- **Claim:** Grid cells furnish a periodic, landmark-independent metric via path integration, modeled by continuous attractor networks driven by velocity/heading; artificial agents trained to navigate develop grid-like codes that support vector navigation. **Sources:** Burak & Fiete, PLoS Computational Biology (2009); Banino et al., Nature (2018). **Support:** direct evidence for the neuroscience/computation claim itself. **Confidence:** high that the models do this; low that it analogizes to the repair.
- **What maps (thin):** intrinsic shape → grid-like reusable metric; absolute position → landmark/anchoring cues. **Why weak:** the mechanism (velocity integration on an attractor manifold producing hexagonal periodicity) has no counterpart in a static positional repair, and periodicity/ring-manifold properties do not transfer. Keep only if the repair has an explicit integration or translation-invariance operator; otherwise cut.
- **What would upgrade it:** demonstrate the intrinsic code obeys a group operation (e.g., translation equivariance or vector composition like grid-based shortcuts in Banino et al.) — then it is substantive; absent that, label decorative.

## Contradictions
- Clean LEC=what / MEC=where vs mixed-coding evidence: the Knierim 2014 framing vs Keene 2016 ("MEC represents locations and, within those, the objects") and Connor & Knierim 2017 review itself noting integration. Record both; do not resolve by picking one.
- Remapping literature (place cells remap across contexts while grids persist) supports separation, but rate-remapping (same place, different firing rate by context) supports binding — both true, so the brain does both separation and conjunction.
