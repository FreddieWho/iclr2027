# Research M4: Margin shrinkage under auxiliary pressure

## Summary
Our pattern — triplet margin 0.64 (frozen recipe) → 0.01–0.17 with routing/InfoNCE/VICReg auxiliaries, pair accuracy mostly intact — is a known signature of uniformity-vs-alignment tension and possible representational compression, not prima facie proof of worse generalization. Classical margin theory (Bartlett et al.) bounds generalization by *normalized* margin, not the raw embedding gap we log, so raw thinning alone supports no generalization claim without norm control and a rank/uniformity check.

## Findings

**1. CAN SAY: ranking-held + gap-thinned is the predicted uniformity–alignment trade-off signature. (Confidence: medium; Support: interpretation of direct evidence)**
- **Claim:** InfoNCE-type auxiliaries explicitly reward global uniformity on the hypersphere alongside local alignment; theory shows contrastive loss asymptotically optimizes both (Wang & Isola 2020). Uniformity pushes *all* points apart, which compresses the *relative* anchor-positive vs anchor-negative gap while preserving order — exactly "margins thin, pair accuracy holds."
- **Sources:** Wang & Isola, "Understanding Contrastive Representation Learning through Alignment and Uniformity on the Hypersphere," ICML 2020 (arXiv:2005.10242; PMLR v119). Alignment = E‖f(x)−f(y)‖² over positives; uniformity = log E exp(−2‖f−f′‖²) over all pairs; contrastive loss shown to asymptotically optimize both.
- **What this licenses:** we may write "thinning with preserved ranking is consistent with increased uniformity pressure from the InfoNCE auxiliary." Researcher inference: applies by analogy to our triplet-margin readout, which Wang & Isola do not study directly.
- **Check that upgrades to high:** log the two Wang & Isola metrics per run; prediction is uniformity loss ↓ (more uniform) and alignment ↑-or-flat in aux runs vs frozen recipe.

**2. MUST AVOID: "smaller raw triplet margin ⇒ worse generalization by margin theory." (Confidence: high; Support: direct evidence)**
- **Claim:** Bartlett et al.'s bound scales with *margin-normalized spectral complexity* (product of spectral norms × correction, divided by margin), for multiclass classifier margins — not raw metric-embedding gaps. A smaller raw gap with smaller Lipschitz norm can leave the bound unchanged or improved.
- **Sources:** Bartlett, Foster & Telgarsky, "Spectrally-Normalized Margin Bounds for Neural Networks," NeurIPS 2017 (arXiv:1706.08498). Bound ∝ spectral complexity / margin; graphical reading via normalized margin distributions (cf. Farhang et al., PMLR 162, discussion of Bartlett).
- **What this forbids:** any sentence like "auxiliaries hurt generalization because margins fell 0.64→0.1." Required instead: report a *normalized* margin (gap divided by product of spectral norms or an empirical Lipschitz estimate) before any generalization statement. Note also a scope gap (researcher inference): Bartlett margins are classifier-output margins; our triplet gap is an embedding-space quantity, so invocation is already analogical.

**3. CAN SAY ONLY WITH A SPECTRUM CHECK: thinning is consistent with partial dimensional collapse, but VICReg-type terms oppose full collapse — do not claim "auxiliaries cause collapse." (Confidence: low–medium; Support: interpretation + conditional prediction)**
- **Claim:** Jing et al. show contrastive SSL embeddings can collapse into a lower-dimensional subspace (decaying singular spectrum driven by augmentation/regularization dynamics; remedy DirectCLR). Concentrated spectra compress absolute pairwise distances, shrinking margins while directions (rankings) survive. That mechanism fits our data, but VICReg's variance hinge + covariance decorrelation are *designed* to prevent collapse (Bardes et al., ICLR 2022), so a blanket collapse attribution is wrong; at most partial/effective-rank compression, or covariance spreading of discriminative energy across decorrelated dims.
- **Sources:** Jing et al., "Understanding Dimensional Collapse in Contrastive Self-Supervised Learning," ICLR 2022 (arXiv:2110.09348); Bardes et al., "VICReg," ICLR 2022 (arXiv:2105.04906 — variance term holds per-dim std above threshold; covariance term decorrelates).
- **Check that decides it:** singular spectrum / effective rank of embeddings per run. Collapse account predicts aux runs show faster singular decay vs frozen recipe, and margins recomputed in a whitened or top-k subspace shrink less. If spectra match, drop the collapse story and keep statement 1 only.

## Contradictions
- VICReg evidence cuts against a naive "all auxiliaries collapse" story: its variance term is a proven anti-collapse mechanism, so InfoNCE (uniformity-driven compression) and VICReg (decorrelation-driven dilution) need distinct micro-explanations despite similar margin numbers. None found beyond this scope tension.

## Missing evidence
- We log raw triplet margins only: no spectral norms, no Wang & Isola alignment/uniformity values, no singular spectra, no temperature ablation for InfoNCE. All three statements above are therefore consistency arguments, not causal proofs, until those three diagnostics are run.
