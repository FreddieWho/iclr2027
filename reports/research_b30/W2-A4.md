# Research: Generative counterparts — diffusion/flow models for formation manifolds (A4)

## Summary
Single-body motion diffusion (MDM, ICLR 2023) and music-conditioned dance diffusion (EDGE, CVPR 2023) provide the direct template: transformer denoisers with masked/editing training. In sports, the closest precedents are conditional trajectory diffusion (MID, CVPR 2022), TacticAI's predictive+generative corner-kick model (Nat. Commun. 2024), and two-stage soccer motion diffusion SMGDiff (ICCV 2025). A formation generator adds three things discriminative probes cannot: (1) counterfactual formation synthesis ("what if"), (2) density-based anomaly scores, (3) principled imputation/forecasting. Cheapest win: a small conditional formation-diffusion inpainter.

## Findings

1. **Claim:** MDM establishes the reusable recipe: classifier-free transformer diffusion over motion sequences with native editing (in-betweening, body-part masking, unconditioned sampling). **Sources:** [MDM project page](https://guytevet.github.io/mdm-page); [arXiv 2209.14916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14916). **Support:** direct evidence (authors state diffusion-adapted to motion domain; ICLR 2023, top-25%). **Confidence:** high. **Evidence grade: A** (peer-reviewed, ICLR 2023). *Relevance (inference):* formation frames = multi-agent analogue of poses; MDM's mask-condition training maps directly to "freeze 19 players, resample 1". Venue/year: ICLR 2023.

2. **Claim:** EDGE shows conditioning + editability scale: transformer diffusion + strong conditioner (Jukebox music features) with joint-wise masking, in-betweening, continuation, while staying physically plausible and beat-faithful. **Sources:** [CVPR 2023 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Tseng_EDGE_Editable_Dance_Generation_From_Music_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf); [arXiv 2211.10658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10658). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. **Evidence grade: A** (CVPR 2023). *Relevance (inference):* replace music conditioning with match context (phase, ball, score); joint-masking becomes player/role-masking for counterfactuals.

3. **Claim:** MID formulates multi-agent trajectory prediction as conditional diffusion: history+social interaction encoded as state embedding, transformer denoiser captures temporal dependencies and multimodality explicitly rather than via one latent. **Sources:** [CVPR 2022 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Gu_Stochastic_Trajectory_Prediction_via_Motion_Indeterminacy_Diffusion_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf); [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.13777). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. **Evidence grade: A** (CVPR 2022). *Relevance (inference):* direct precedent for stochastic formation futures + distributional anomaly scores.

4. **Claim:** TacticAI is the closest sports-formation generative precedent: graph model of corner kicks with predictive (receiver/shot) + generative (sample alternative setups, rank by predicted success) components; expert study preferred AI setups ~90% of the time. **Sources:** [Nature Commun. paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-45965-x); [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/tacticai-ai-assistant-for-football-tactics). **Support:** direct evidence for architecture and evaluation; 90% preference is author/expert-study reported, small-sample qualitative. **Confidence:** medium-high. **Evidence grade: A-** (peer-reviewed Nat. Commun. 2024, vol. 15:1906; discount qualitative claim). Venue/year: Nature Communications 2024.

5. **Claim:** SMGDiff (two-stage: trajectory diffusion + autoregressive body-motion diffusion, real-time controllable soccer animation) and GenTac (diffusion over continuous multi-player trajectories for tactics forecasting) confirm diffusion-on-soccer-data is active but focused on animation/forecasting, not formation-manifold density. **Sources:** [SMGDiff ICCV 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Yang_SMGDiff_Soccer_Motion_Generation_using_Diffusion_Probabilistic_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html); [GenTac arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11786). **Support:** direct evidence for SMGDiff; GenTac is a 40-page 2026 technical report, unverified. **Confidence:** medium (SMGDiff), low (GenTac). **Evidence grades: A- (ICCV 2025) / C (GenTac preprint).**

6. **Claim:** Flow matching offers faster sampling (~10 vs ~1000 steps) with competitive quality (Motion Flow Matching reports SOTA FID on text-to-motion) and ODE-based likelihoods usable as anomaly scores. **Sources:** [MFM page](https://taohu.me/mfm); [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ikdB0VXPlw); [HY-Motion 1.0 flow-matching scale-up](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23464v1). **Support:** interpretation (claims from project reports/preprints, not yet independently replicated in sports). **Confidence:** medium-low. **Evidence grade: B.** *Researcher inference:* attractive for extension 3 but least mature for 20-agent pitch data.

7. **Claim (inference):** Beyond discriminative probing, a formation generator uniquely enables: (a) counterfactuals with plausibility guardrails, (b) density/rarity scores for anomaly detection, (c) missing-player imputation and short-horizon forecasting under one model. Discriminative probes test "does representation contain X"; generative models test "what formations are probable under the learned manifold". **Support:** researcher inference grounded in 1–5. **Confidence:** medium. **Evidence grade: C.**

## Ranked generative extensions (cost/benefit)

**E1 (do first) — Conditional formation inpainter (masked diffusion).** Train a small transformer denoiser on 20×T×2 windows with role/team/ball conditioning and MDM/EDGE-style random masking (mask players, time spans, spatial zones). Yields counterfactual generation + imputation + continuation with one objective. Cost: low (reuses existing coordinate tensors, no new labels; SE(2)/flip augmentation handles pitch symmetry). Benefit: high (directly answers "what-if" coaching questions; TacticAI-style setup ranking becomes possible). Evidence: A.
**E2 (do second) — Conditional trajectory-diffusion forecaster + rarity scorer.** MID-style: encode history+interactions, diffuse over futures; anomaly = reconstruction/conditional-sample distance or likelihood proxy, tail-risk distributions (cf. Monte-Carlo pass-search use). Cost: medium (needs temporal windows, calibration of rarity thresholds). Benefit: medium-high (density-based anomaly detection probes cannot do). Evidence: A for method, B for anomaly-via-diffusion in sports.
**E3 (defer) — Flow-matching / latent prior for fast sampling + ODE likelihood.** Distill or re-train E1/E2 objective as flow matching for 10-step sampling and exact-ish likelihoods. Cost: high (new training objective, stability tuning; sports evidence thin). Benefit: medium (speed + cleaner anomaly scores; only pays off once E1/E2 validated). Evidence: B.

Manifold notes (inference): treat formation as 40-D/frame with permutation structure — condition on role channels rather than enforcing full equivariance at first; enforce pitch bounds and team-separation softly via conditioning/augmentation before hard constraints.

## Contradictions
None on core method facts. Tension: TacticAI's strong expert-preference claim vs. narrow scope (corners only, Premier League single-club data) — do not generalize to open-play formations. GenTac's broad "tactics as stochastic process" claim is unverified preprint vs. TacticAI's peer-reviewed narrow claim.

## Missing evidence
No peer-reviewed formation-manifold density/anomaly benchmark found; rarity-score calibration for 20-agent data is open. No head-to-head diffusion vs. flow-matching comparison on sports tracking data. Ball–player joint generation (SMGDiff's contact-matrix trick) for formations untested.
