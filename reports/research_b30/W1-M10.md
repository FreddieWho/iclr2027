# Research: Strength of evidence for independent-provider replication (T5R6, n=8)

## Summary
T5R6's 8/8 same-direction results on 8 independent-provider (SoccerTrack-v2) matches clear the exact two-sided sign-test threshold (p≈0.008 each) and satisfy the strongest common replication standard — a geographically/provider-independent external check. It licenses a directional-consistency claim under provider shift, not a magnitude, calibration, or broad-generalizability claim, and reviewers will weight it as supportive but small-n evidence. Overall grade: **moderate (directional) / weak (quantitative)**.

## Findings

**1. Claim: Held-out ≠ independent; T5R6 meets the "independent external" bar under standard taxonomies.** Internal held-out/cross-validation reuses the same collection pipeline and cannot detect pipeline leakage or provider idiosyncrasy; TRIPOD+AI (BMJ 2024) and the BMJ external-validation framework reserve "external validation" for data not used in any development step, grading temporal < geographic/provider-independent transportability highest. An 8-match set from a different provider (SoccerTrack-v2) with no tuning on it is geographic/provider validation, one step above a same-provider holdout. It directly answers the Kapoor & Narayanan (Patterns 2023) leakage critique — that test-set reuse and preprocessing leakage inflate in-distribution results — provided no T5R6 thresholds were re-tuned on SoccerTrack-v2. **Sources:** Collins et al., TRIPOD+AI, BMJ 2024 (bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378); Riley et al., Evaluation of clinical prediction models part 1, BMJ 2024;384:e074819; Kapoor & Narayanan, Patterns 4(9):100804 (2023). **Support:** interpretation (taxonomy applied to stated T5R6 design). **Confidence:** high on taxonomy; medium that T5R6 had zero tuning leakage (unverified from brief alone — must assert explicitly in paper).

**2. Claim: Exact statistical reading — each 8/8 is p≈0.004 one-sided / ≈0.008 two-sided; but the sign test at n=8 is coarse and low-powered.** Under H0 (per-match direction is a coin flip), P(8/8)=(1/2)^8=1/256≈0.0039 one-sided; two-sided p=2/256=1/128≈0.0078 (standard exact binomial/sign-test calculation). Note the granularity: at n=8 the only outcome significant at two-sided α=0.05 is 8/8 (7/8 gives two-sided p=18/256≈0.070, n.s.; one-sided 9/256≈0.035). Power is therefore weak except for near-deterministic effects: one-sided power (reject if ≥7/8) is only ~26% if true per-match agreement prob.=0.7, ~50% at 0.8; two-sided power (reject only at 8/8) at true 0.8 is just ~17%. Researcher inference: passing despite low power is encouraging for direction but the design could only ever establish sign, never size; report CIs accordingly (e.g., exact Clopper–Pearson 95% CI for 8/8 is ~[0.63, 1.00] — compatible with a true agreement rate as low as ~63%). Multiple-endpoint note: with 4 metrics + intervention read-out (5 sign checks), each at 0.0078, Bonferroni family-wise p≈0.039 still <0.05, but the metrics are correlated so do not multiply p-values into a joint "all-5" claim; present per-metric tests plus a pre-specified primary. **Sources:** Sign-test/binomial exact calculation (any standard text; e.g., Wikipedia "Sign test"; r-tutor/StatsDirect binomial-test references); power arithmetic is researcher computation from binomial probabilities. **Support:** direct evidence (math) + researcher inference (wording guidance). **Confidence:** high on p-values; high on coarseness/power numbers.

**3. Claim: Provider shift is the right framing, and it licenses direction only — magnitude will shift, per the dataset-reproduction literature; reviewers treat this as a plus, not proof.** "Provider shift" (new sensor/annotation/tracking pipeline, same nominal task) is a case of dataset/distribution shift (Quiñonero-Candela et al., MIT Press 2009): even faithful reproductions show performance drops while preserving rankings — Recht et al. (ICML 2019) built new CIFAR-10/ImageNet test sets following the original pipelines and found 3–15% accuracy drops with preserved model ordering ("accuracy on the line," extended Miller et al., ICML 2021). So the correct T5R6 expectation and claim is directional preservation under shift, not numeric transport of effect sizes or thresholds. Reviewer weighting (ML venues; NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks reviewer guidance 2025–26 stresses independent documentation/reproducibility; TRIPOD+AI explicitly encourages external validation): independent-provider directional confirmation is consistently treated as meaningful robustness evidence that upgrades a paper from "in-distribution only," but with n=8 reviewers will cap it as small-sample, single-alternative-provider support — sufficient for "consistent direction across providers," insufficient for "generalizes broadly" or deployment-readiness. Must disclose: single external provider, n=8, direction-only pre-registration (if any), no re-tuning, and any SoccerTrack-v2 quirks (tracking noise, league/style mix). **Sources:** Recht et al., ICML (PMLR 97) 2019; Miller et al., PMLR 139 (ICML 2021); Quiñonero-Candela et al., Dataset Shift in ML, 2009; NeurIPS Evaluations & Datasets Reviewer Guidelines 2026. **Support:** interpretation applied to T5R6. **Confidence:** high on literature pattern; medium on predicted reviewer weighting (venue- and reviewer-dependent).

## The 3 concrete statements about OUR T5R6 (use verbatim-adapted)
1. "On 8 matches from an independent provider (SoccerTrack-v2), all 4 primary metrics and the intervention effect agreed in direction with the main results (8/8 each; exact two-sided sign-test p=1/128≈0.008 per check; 95% Clopper–Pearson CI for 8/8 ≈ [0.63, 1.00])."
2. "This licenses a directional-consistency-under-provider-shift claim only; it does not license transport of effect magnitudes, thresholds, or calibration, and does not establish generalizability beyond the two providers studied (single external source, n=8, sign-only power)."
3. "Design statement to include: the SoccerTrack-v2 set was fixed before evaluation, with no model/threshold tuning on it (key reference: TRIPOD+AI, Collins et al., BMJ 2024; leakage standard: Kapoor & Narayanan, Patterns 2023)."

## Contradictions
None found on the core statistics or taxonomy. Tension noted, not contradiction: TRIPOD-style guidance values independent validation highly, while the Recht/Miller literature warns even good reproductions show level drops — resolved by claiming direction, not magnitude.

## Missing evidence
- Whether any threshold, normalization, or model choice was adjusted after seeing SoccerTrack-v2 (decides leakage-free status).
- Which check was pre-specified primary vs. secondary (affects multiplicity reading).
- SoccerTrack-v2 match characteristics (league, tracking quality) bounding the shift claim.
