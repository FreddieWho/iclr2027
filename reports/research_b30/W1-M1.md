# Research M1: Layer-wise probing — where do geometric vs task-relevant signals live?

## Summary
Linear per-layer probes (Alain & Bengio lineage) plus BERT-layer analogues consistently find a **surface→syntax→semantics gradient**: raw/local features decode best early, relational/abstract features peak mid-to-late. In GNNs this gradient competes with **oversmoothing**: deep layers exponentially collapse toward topology-only subspaces (Oono & Suzuki 2020), so geometric detail can *decrease* with depth. For our 20-player graph-attention encoder this predicts: displacement geometry peaks early/mid, response/Jacobian-relevant integration peaks later, with a depth ceiling set by homogenization.

## What the literature says

**1. Linear classifier probes (method).**
Alain & Bengio (ICLR 2017) attach a linear classifier to each frozen intermediate layer, trained without touching the backbone. Claim: linear decodability per layer tracks how "immediately usable" a representation is; rising probe accuracy = progressive linearization of task information. **Source:** arXiv:1610.01644. **Support:** direct evidence (method definition). **Confidence:** high. Researcher inference: linear (not MLP) probes are the right default because they test the representation, not the probe; add a control-task selectivity check (Hewitt & Liang style) to avoid probe-memorization confounds.

**2. BERT-layer analogue (pipeline ordering).**
Tenney et al. (ACL 2019, "BERT Rediscovers the Classical NLP Pipeline") use edge probing + learned per-layer mixing weights: POS peaks low, constituency/dependency mid, SRL/coreference high — expected-layer rises along the classical pipeline. Jawahar et al. (ACL 2019) replicate the stratification across 17 tasks: surface/phrase-level low, syntax (tree depth, agreement) middle, semantics high. **Sources:** ACL P19-1452; ACL P19-1356. **Support:** direct evidence for Transformers/text. **Confidence:** high within NLP, medium for transfer to spatial GNNs. Researcher inference: the analogue for us is raw position → local relations (marking, adjacency) → global embedding-shift/response.

**3. GNN depth / oversmoothing interaction.**
Oono & Suzuki (ICLR 2020) prove GCN node features exponentially lose expressive power with depth, converging to a subspace determined only by graph topology and degrees (rate tied to spectral gap); Li et al. (AAAI 2018) and the Dirichlet-energy literature give the measurable form: neighbor-difference energy E(X)→0 and pairwise cosine→1 in deep layers. Attention (GAT) slows but does not remove this. **Source:** arXiv:1905.10947. **Support:** direct (theorem + experiments) for GCN-class models. **Confidence:** high for the mechanism, medium for exact onset depth in a small dense 20-node sports graph (dense graphs smooth *faster* — expect ceiling at few layers). Researcher inference: any layer-probing curve that rises then falls for geometric decoding is the predicted oversmoothing signature, not a bug.

## Ranked experiments for OUR setting (graph attention, 20 positions/frame)

**E1 (do first): Per-layer dual linear probes — geometry vs response.**
*Measure at each layer (frozen):* (a) geometric probes — linear regression of displaced-player coordinates/displacement magnitude and per-node Jacobian norm from that layer's node states; (b) task probes — linear prediction of final-frame embedding shift (‖Δh‖, cosine shift) and the Spearman-0.5–0.85 response target from pooled layer states. Report R²/Spearman per layer + selectivity vs. shuffled-position control.
*Patterns:* geometry R² peaking at layers 1–2 and falling after = local encoding then mixing (expected); task score rising monotonically to the top = progressive integration (healthy); task peaking mid then dropping = oversmoothing/compression ceiling — stop adding depth. Crossing point (geometry↓ while task↑) localizes the geometry→task transform and tells the dual-channel repair where to split context vs intrinsic.
*Ref:* Alain & Bengio 2017 (ICLR) method; Tenney et al. 2019 (ACL) gradient logic.

**E2: Layer-wise sensitivity alignment + homogenization diagnostic.**
*Measure:* per-layer mean ‖∂h_layer/∂x_i‖ (or finite-difference displacement→activation shift) correlated against final embedding shift, plus Dirichlet energy and mean pairwise node cosine per layer.
*Patterns:* sensitivity–final-shift Spearman rising with depth = late layers faithfully integrate small displacements (validates Jacobian probing); flat/falling + energy→0 and cosine→1 = oversmoothed deep layers destroying intrinsic signal — predicts repair should read out from the pre-collapse layer, and bounds encoder depth.
*Ref:* Oono & Suzuki 2020 (ICLR).

**E3: Context-vs-intrinsic separation per layer (attention-scale probe).**
*Measure:* per-layer attention entropy / effective neighborhood radius, and paired probes with full-graph vs. intrinsic-only (self-node/ablated-neighbor) inputs.
*Patterns:* early layers local/low-entropy with intrinsic-only ≈ full-graph geometry = intrinsic channel lives low; deep layers global/high-entropy with full-graph ≫ intrinsic-only task score = context channel lives high. If deep layers show high entropy *without* task gain, attention is mixing without informing — prune or regularize those heads.
*Ref:* Jawahar et al. 2019 (ACL) stratification + GAT/oversmoothing literature.

## Contradictions / caveats
- Probes can memorize (probe capacity confound); BERT gradient partly reflects pre-training objective, not universal law — our sports encoder may compress differently. Mitigate with linear-only probes + control tasks.
- Dense 20-node complete-ish graphs oversmooth faster than sparse citation graphs Oono & Suzuki studied; exact collapse depth does not transfer — measure E2 rather than assuming.
- None found on core claims (probe method, pipeline ordering, exponential smoothing loss).

## Missing evidence
- No published per-layer probe curves for sports-pose GAT encoders; E1–E3 baselines must be established in-house.
- Exact attention-smoothing rate for GAT on dense small graphs not verified; E2's energy curve is the needed measurement.

## Evidence grade: MODERATE
Strong method + strong NLP-layering evidence, but transfer to shallow dense sports GNNs is inferential until E1/E2 curves are measured.
