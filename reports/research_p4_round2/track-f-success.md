# Research: Successful joint invariance + discriminative training on shared trunks

## Summary
Every durable success at joint invariance + discrimination on one trunk uses **asymmetry between the two branches** (momentum teacher and/or predictor + stop-grad) plus an **explicit anti-collapse operator on teacher outputs** (centering+sharpening, entropy maximization, or KoLeo spread) — never two symmetric heads pulling one trunk. The second consistent pattern is **weight/schedule subordination**: the contrastive/discriminative term is kept tiny (∼0.03–0.1) or delayed, and SupCon-style sequential training (contrast, then linear CE) beats simultaneous co-training. All three top ingredients are things our 15 collapsed runs have not tried.

## Findings

**1. DINO (Caron et al., ICCV 2021): collapse is prevented by centering + sharpening on a momentum teacher, not by predictor or negatives.**
Student and teacher share architecture; teacher is EMA of student (momentum cosine 0.996→1.0). Teacher outputs get *centering* (bias c, EMA update m≈0.9, prevents one-dominant-dimension collapse) and *sharpening* (teacher temp warmed 0.04→0.07 in first 30 epochs; student temp fixed 0.1, prevents uniform collapse). Ablation: either operation alone collapses; together they train stably, and the authors explicitly report predictor, advanced normalization, and contrastive loss add little once centering+sharpening + momentum teacher exist. Multi-crop (2 global + 8–10 local views, local-to-global prediction) adds several points and is part of the working recipe; small patches further help. [Summary/ablation](https://mchromiak.github.io/articles/2021/May/03/DINO-Emerging-Properties-in-Self-Supervised-Vision-Transformers/) · [centering/sharpening detail](http://aidventure.es/posts/dino) · [both-essential ablation note](https://www.abhik.ai/papers/dino)

**2. BYOL (Grill et al., NeurIPS 2020) + SimSiam isolate the load-bearing piece: the predictor, with stop-grad, is what breaks symmetry.**
BYOL has no negatives; it minimizes similarity of two augmented views using an online branch with an extra predictor MLP plus a momentum target (τ cosine 0.996→1.0, batch 4096, 300–1000 epochs). Removing the predictor collapses the model *even with* the momentum teacher — predictor asymmetry is load-bearing, momentum alone is not sufficient. SimSiam (Chen & He, CVPR 2021) keeps predictor + stop-grad, drops the momentum encoder entirely, and stays within 0.8–2.0% of full performance down to batch 64–128 — directly relevant to our batch-64 regime. Caveat from theory: Li et al. (ECCV 2022) show non-contrastive Siamese nets partially collapse when the model is *underparameterized relative to dataset size*, with continual training as one fix — a warning for our 140–190k-param trunk. [BYOL predictor/momentum analysis](https://ru00ys-lab.com/blog/byol) · [SimSiam batch-size robustness](https://learnopencv.com/simsiam) · [underparameterization collapse](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136910476.pdf)

**3. Masked-prediction variants (iBOT, MSN, data2vec, DINOv2) show the stable way to add a "recoverability" objective: predict masked views from an EMA teacher, reuse one head, regularize output entropy.**
- *iBOT (Zhou et al., CVPR 2022)*: keeps the DINO cross-view loss and adds masked-patch prediction through the *same* projection head (online tokenizer = teacher). MIM + self-distillation stabilize each other. [Lightly iBOT docs](https://docs.lightly.ai/self-supervised-learning/examples/ibot.html)
- *MSN (Assran et al., ECCV 2022)*: masked anchor → unmasked target; backprop only through the anchor; replaces centering/sharpening with a mean-entropy-maximization (me-max) regularizer on assignments. Heavy masking of the anchor is itself an invariance load. [MSN paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.07141) · [anchor-only backprop note](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2022/papers_ECCV/papers/136910442.pdf)
- *data2vec (Baevski et al., ICML 2022)*: one recipe for speech/vision/NLP — regress contextualized teacher targets (averaged top-K layers, normalized) of the *full* input from a *masked* view, teacher = EMA. No discretization, no contrastive term, no collapse. Closest published analog of "recoverability-style aux loss done right." [data2vec](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03555)
- *DINOv2 (Oquab et al., TMLR 2024)*: DINO image-level + iBOT patch-level + KoLeo spread regularizer (uniform feature spread within batch). Loss ablation: KoLeo helps retrieval, iBOT term helps dense tasks; neither destroys the trunk because each acts on a different granularity/head. [DINOv2 loss ablation](https://debuggercafe.com/dinov2-visual-feature-learning-without-supervision) · [combined-objective tour](https://storrs.io/dinov2-explained)

**4. Hybrid supervised+SSL: sequential beats simultaneous; when simultaneous, keep the contrastive weight ∼0.03 and delay it.**
- *SupCon (Khosla et al., NeurIPS 2020)* — the canonical supervised+contrastive win (ResNet-50 ImageNet 76.5% vs 75.3% CE; ResNet-200 81.4%) — is explicitly **two-stage**: SupCon pretraining, *then* frozen-trunk linear CE. It never co-trains both losses on one trunk. Lesson: if joint training collapses, the published fallback is sequential. [SupCon results](https://github.com/HobbitLong/SupContrast) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11362)
- *CAN / CMAE (contrastive + MAE)*: naively adding contrastive to MAE gives only +0.2%; gains need pixel-shift augmentation (+0.5%) and a feature decoder (+0.2%). Reference CAN recipe: `weight_mae 0.97 / weight_simclr 0.03`, batch 64×4-accum, 50 epochs — i.e., the discriminative term is a 3% nudge, not a partner. [CAN recipe](https://github.com/shlokk/mae-contrastive) · [CMAE ablations](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2207.13532)
- *ACMAE (CS231n 2025, CIFAR-100 ViT)*: contrastive weight λ_max=0.05 with **delay D=100 + warmup W=100 epochs** — the trunk learns structure first, discrimination late. Direct template for our 80-epoch schedule (scale to delay ∼30–40). [ACMAE report](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2025/papers/CS231N%20-%20ACMAE%20-%20Karan%20Singh.pdf)

**5. Minimum conditions for our regime (~150k params, batch 64, 80 epochs, no teacher yet): evidence says batch 64 is fine, but no-teacher + short schedule + tiny model is the danger zone.**
SimSiam-class methods tolerate batch 64; small-batch BYOL needs stabilized normalization (momentum/synced BN stats recover SOTA at batch 128 without TPUs). EMA scaling work (NeurIPS 2023) shows teacher momentum must be re-tuned with batch size/epochs (baseline ρ=0.99 at batch 4096), and short schedules want *faster* teachers (lower τ start, e.g. 0.99 not 0.996). Combined with Li et al.'s underparameterization-collapse result, our regime's risk is specifically symmetric co-training on a small trunk with no slow teacher — exactly what we ran 15 times. [small-batch momentum teacher](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.07525) · [EMA scaling](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/e7681dd6fe16052433ab68cd1555bdc9-Paper-Conference.pdf)

## Ranked transferable ingredients (none tried yet)

**#1 — Momentum teacher + predictor + stop-grad (BYOL/data2vec asymmetry). Venue: NeurIPS 2020 / ICML 2022.**
*Mechanism*: teacher = EMA of student (no grad); student branch gets a 2-layer predictor MLP the teacher lacks; loss only pulls student→teacher. Breaks the symmetric co-adaptation in which both heads jointly drive shared channels to zero.
*Starting points*: τ cosine 0.99→1.0 over 80 epochs (faster than DINO's 0.996 — short schedule needs faster teacher); predictor 2× (dim→4×dim→dim) with BN/ReLU; stop-grad on teacher always; aux loss on predictor output, CE on trunk/head as now.
*Why it breaks our pattern*: our collapse is symmetric-gradient destruction; asymmetry + slow target is the one intervention with ablations showing collapse-without-it.

**#2 — Teacher-output balancing: centering + sharpening (or me-max) instead of trunk variance penalties. Venue: ICCV 2021 (DINO) / ECCV 2022 (MSN).**
*Mechanism*: center teacher logits with EMA (m=0.9) and sharpen with low temperature (teacher 0.04→0.07 warmup 30 epochs, student/anchor 0.1); or MSN's me-max entropy penalty on assignments. Acts on *outputs*, leaving trunk channels unpenalized — unlike VICReg-style variance/covariance terms that can directly zero channels.
*Starting points*: implement on the aux head only; monitor teacher entropy (collapse diagnostics: entropy→0 = sharpening-only dominance; entropy→log K = centering-only uniformity).
*Why it breaks our pattern*: prior brief's failures all regularize the trunk; DINO/MSN prove output-space balancing suffices.

**#3 — Subordinate-and-delay the discriminative/recoverability term; keep sequential fallback. Venues: NeurIPS 2020 (SupCon), TR 2022 (CAN), 2025 (ACMAE).**
*Mechanism*: contrastive/recoverability weight 0.03–0.10, zero for first ~40% of epochs then linear warmup; if joint still collapses, go SupCon-sequential (aux pretrain → freeze → linear CE).
*Starting points*: w_aux=0.05, delay 30 epochs of 80, warmup 10; multi-crop equivalent: 2 strong + 4 cheap (flip/crop/noise) views per sample at batch 64 — affordable local-to-global signal without new data.
*Why it breaks our pattern*: published joint wins all subordinate one loss; our runs co-trained near-equal weights from epoch 0.

## Gaps
Could not confirm a published success at *our exact* scale (150k params + 80 epochs + joint CE from epoch 0) — smallest clean wins are CIFAR-scale ViTs/ResNet-18s with hundreds of epochs. Next step: 2×2 pilot (momentum teacher on/off × w_aux 1.0/0.05) at batch 64 with teacher-entropy logging; ~4 short runs would discriminate ingredient #1 vs #3.
