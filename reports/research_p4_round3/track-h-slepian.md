# Research: Slepian μ as predictor + concentration-threshold for graph probing

## Summary
No published work uses graph-Slepian concentration eigenvalue μ as a covariate/predictor of downstream task success — μ and Shannon number K appear only as construction diagnostics and sampling/reconstruction guarantees. That absence is the opportunity: vertex–frequency literature already has accepted templates for presenting localization limits as positive results (feasibility regions, concentration–bandwidth tradeoff curves), and a pre-registered logistic/ROC analysis of success-on-μ with band/support controls converts the low-μ cells into a theory-predicts-experiment claim.

## Findings
1. **μ/K are diagnostics and sampling bounds, never performance predictors — the gap is real.** Tsitsvero–Barbarossa–Di Lorenzo (IEEE TSP 2016) define exactly your operator C=P_b S P_b, Slepian vectors as energy-maximizers in node subset S for band b, eigenvalues μ∈[0,1], Shannon number K=Σμ=tr(C) as effective dimension; results are uncertainty bounds and sampling/reconstruction guarantees (how many samples K demands), with no plot of downstream accuracy vs μ. [TSP paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7473812) [EUSIPCO degrees-of-freedom version](https://doi.org/10.1109/eusipco.2015.7362635). Van De Ville et al. ("When Slepian meets Fiedler," 2017) use concentration-vs-bandwidth tradeoff plots and C. elegans guided-embedding demos as the presentation device — limit-as-design-tool, still no μ→performance regression. [arXiv html](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.08401v2) [UNIGE record](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:104890) [C. elegans follow-up](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.04657). Classical Slepian references confirm eigenvalues λ_k literally are "spectral concentration ratios." [DPSS reference](https://www.geostatistics.tuc.gr/fileadmin/users_data/geostatistics/papers/Hristopulos2021_ReferenceWorkEntry_DiscreteProlateSpheroidalSeque.pdf). Implication: a success-vs-μ threshold plot has no direct prior art to collide with — frame as first μ-as-routability-predictor test.
2. **Borrowable plot/framing templates for "limit as result."** (a) Agaskar–Lu 2013 spectral graph uncertainty principle: feasible (vertex-spread, spectral-spread) region plot — points below a curve are declared impossible, and the curve itself is the theorem figure. [arXiv:1206.6356](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.6356) [HTML survey](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.03030v1). (b) Shuman et al. vertex-frequency frames / windowed graph Fourier frames: Heisenberg-box tiling diagrams showing each atom's vertex×spectrum footprint; delocalized atoms drawn as wide boxes are a feature of the dictionary, not a failure. [Vertex-frequency analysis](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.1307.5708) [ETH collection](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/bitstreams/94a88e44-bdb4-4d3b-afaf-9ca7a47969e5/download). (c) Hammond et al. 2009/2011 spectral graph wavelets: scale-parameterized localization curves (wavelet at scale s has footprint f(s)) used to justify which scales are probed. [arXiv:0912.3848](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.3848). Recipe for you: x-axis μ (or K per cell), y-axis routing success; overlay the analytic "no-route" region (μ<μ* shaded) exactly like an uncertainty feasibility region, plus per-cell Heisenberg-style tiles. Title the figure "Concentration predicts routability" not "delocalization limits performance."
3. **Threshold statistics with 30–60 sets/cell: hierarchical logistic + ROC + stratified rank test.** Right analysis: (i) trial-level mixed-effects logistic regression success ~ μ + band + support-size + snapshot-difficulty + (1|snapshot), testing μ coefficient and μ×band interaction — controls the three named confounds and the repeated-snapshot structure; report odds ratio per 0.1 μ. (ii) ROC/AUC of μ-alone as routability classifier with Youden-J threshold μ* and bootstrap CI — this is the citable "threshold" object; standard method with confound-adjusted (standardized/weighted) ROC extensions available. [ROC/AUC primer](https://www.numberanalytics.com/blog/roc-auc-logistic-regression-primer) [ROC review, Hajian-Tilaki 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3755824/) [covariate-adjusted ROC](https://https-sage-cnpereading-com-443.webvpn1.xju.edu.cn/doi/10.1177/0962280217702416). (iii) Stratified Spearman success-vs-μ within each (band, support) cell as robustness check against functional-form assumptions. Pre-register μ* prediction (e.g. K<1 → near-chance) before computing success to make it theory-driven; control snapshot difficulty via baseline (ε=0 or shuffled-S) success as covariate, and never pool across bands without band fixed effects since μ distributions differ by band by construction.

## Gaps
Could not find any paper plotting downstream task success vs graph-Slepian μ (searched combined backends; negative result stated confidently but not exhaustively proven). Exact analytic μ* for routing (vs sampling K) is not in the literature — propose it empirically via Youden-J and present as measured bound, or derive a leakage-based bound (routed energy ≤ μ·ε leaks (1−μ)·ε off-target) as the theory curve. Next step: run the pre-registered logistic+ROC on existing logged μ before new experiments.

## Prescriptions (ranked, ≤3)
1. **μ-threshold figure + hierarchical logistic (do first).** Analysis: mixed logistic success~μ+band+support+baseline+(1|snapshot); figure: binned success vs μ with fitted curve, shaded μ<μ* no-route region, per-cell K annotated. Why positive: pre-registered monotone prediction confirmed = theory win; low cells become "as predicted." Key refs: Tsitsvero et al. TSP 2016; Agaskar–Lu 2013 (feasibility-region framing).
2. **ROC/AUC routability certificate (one number for abstract).** Analysis: μ-alone ROC, AUC + Youden μ* with bootstrap CI; report stratified-by-band AUCs. Why positive: threshold-independent evidence μ discriminates routable/non-routable, standard clinical-diagnostic prestige format. Key refs: Hajian-Tilaki ROC review 2013; Shuman frames (dictionary-characterization precedent).
3. **Leakage-bound overlay (theory curve, optional if time).** Analysis: plot observed success against analytic ceiling from off-target leakage (1−μ)·ε at fixed ε=0.25; check high-band small-support points sit under ceiling. Why positive: converts "delocalized" into information-theoretic bound no method can beat. Key refs: Van De Ville 2017 (tradeoff-curve precedent); Hammond 2011 (scale–localization curves).
