This is one pooling-accessibility switch in the Phase-GAT family. The phase-label heldout set is small and label coverage is reported. A response change without an objective task improvement is representation shaping, not repair. No claim is made for all architectures or for AMR until the P3 gate is reviewed. The response-blind node-sensitivity localization diagnostic has q-full parity below 1e-8, but mean top-k recall is only 0.1546 for relational pairwise and 0.1487 for team mean against a 0.1458 support-size baseline. This is weak localization enrichment, not evidence of a useful localization task or task-level repair.
